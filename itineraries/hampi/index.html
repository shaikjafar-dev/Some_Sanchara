Preparation and safety

Stay with the group

Keep to the group plan, especially on the rocky hills and at sunrise.

Dress for temples

Wear modest, comfortable clothes and be ready to remove footwear at temples.

Beat the heat

Hampi gets hot. Carry water, a cap and sun protection, and take shade breaks.

Mind the rocks

Boulders can be slippery. Wear shoes with good grip for the hill climbs.

Respect the heritage

Do not climb on or mark the monuments, and take your rubbish with you.
