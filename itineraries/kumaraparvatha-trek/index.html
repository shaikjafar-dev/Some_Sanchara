Preparation and safety

Stay with the group

Follow the trek lead’s instructions and never leave the trail on your own.

Be honest about fitness

Moderate fitness helps. Tell the team about any health condition before you travel.

Expect changing weather

Ghats weather turns quickly. Pack rain cover for your bag and expect wet, slippery ground in the monsoon.

Pace and hydrate

Sip water often, take the breaks and tell someone early if you feel unwell.

Leave no trace

Carry your waste back out and keep the forest as you found it.
