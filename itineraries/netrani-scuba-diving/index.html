Stay safe

Diving preparation and safety

Safety instructions

Listen to the dive team

Follow your instructor’s briefing and hand signals throughout the dive.

Share your health details

Tell the team about asthma, heart, ear or sinus problems, pregnancy or any medication before you book.

Skip alcohol before diving

Avoid alcohol the night before and the morning of your dive, and sleep well.

Look, do not touch

Keep your hands off the coral and marine life to protect the reef and yourself.

Sea conditions come first

Weather and sea state can change the plan. The dive team makes the final call.
