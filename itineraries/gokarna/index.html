Preparation and safety

Stay with the group

Keep together on the beach trek and at the falls and caves.

Respect the sea

Swim only where the group lead says it is safe, and never alone.

Footing matters

Rocks and forest paths are slippery when wet. Wear shoes with grip for treks.

Stay hydrated

The coast is humid. Drink water often and use sun protection.

Keep it clean

Carry your waste back out of beaches, forest and caves.
