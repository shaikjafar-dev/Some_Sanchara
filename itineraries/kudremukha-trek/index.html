Preparation and safety

Stay with the group

Follow the trek lead and the permit rules, and never leave the trail on your own.

Be trek-fit

This is a moderate to difficult trek of about 20–22 km. Build stamina beforehand and tell the team about any health condition.

Weather changes fast

Mist, wind and rain can arrive suddenly on open ridges. Carry rain cover and a warm layer.

Pace and hydrate

Start steady, drink often and speak up early if you feel unwell.

Leave no trace

Carry all waste back and respect the forest rules.
