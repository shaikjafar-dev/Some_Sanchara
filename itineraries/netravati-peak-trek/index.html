Preparation and safety

Stay with the group

Follow the trek lead and keep to the trail, especially near waterfalls and slopes.

Moderate fitness helps

This is a moderate trek of roughly 10–14 km. Tell the team about any health condition.

Weather changes fast

Mist and rain can arrive suddenly. Carry rain cover and expect slippery ground.

Pace and hydrate

Sip water often and speak up early if you feel unwell.

Leave no trace

Carry all waste back and keep the hills clean.
